Frequently asked questions

Quick answers before you start.

Is I Love Doc Converter really free?

Yes. There is no account and no charge for the tools on this page.

What happens to my files?

Files are processed in a temporary workspace and deleted once your result is sent. Please avoid uploading highly confidential documents.

What is the size limit?

You can upload up to 20 files, with a combined size of 100 MB per conversion.

My PDF is scanned. What should I do?

Use “OCR PDF to text” to extract text from scanned pages. Scanned PDFs cannot be converted directly to Word or Excel with good accuracy.

Why does the converted layout look different?

Results depend on how the original file is built. Complex layouts and tables may need a quick manual check.
